Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how AEGIS AllTerrain and AEGIS Sales OS handle personal information when people contact us, request quotes, interact with our sales workflows, or connect with our LinkedIn integration.

Information we collect

We may collect business contact details such as name, company name, email address, phone number, enquiry details, product interest, marketplace enquiry details, and publicly available company or profile information relevant to a sales conversation.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, manage stock and quotes, follow up with prospects, improve our sales process, create approved business content, and keep records of business communications.

LinkedIn integration

Where a LinkedIn account is connected, AEGIS Sales OS may use authorised LinkedIn access to prepare, schedule, or publish approved business content on behalf of the connected account. We do not sell LinkedIn account data.

Sharing information

We only share information where needed to operate the business, provide a requested service, maintain our systems, comply with the law, or work with trusted service providers such as hosting, email, CRM, analytics, and automation platforms.

Retention

We keep business enquiry and communication records for as long as needed to manage customer relationships, stock enquiries, sales follow-up, accounting, legal obligations, and system security.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct, delete, or stop using your personal information where applicable. You can also disconnect third-party integrations such as LinkedIn through the relevant account or app settings.
