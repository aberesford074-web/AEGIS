AEGIS DEALER OS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

AEGIS Dealer OS helps equipment dealers manage customers, machines, sales, activities and approved communications. Each dealer workspace is kept separate from every other dealer.

Information we process

We process the business records a dealer adds to AEGIS, account and workspace details, and connection details for services the dealer chooses to connect.

Google user data

When a dealer connects Gmail, AEGIS receives the Google account identity and permission to send email only after the user approves Google's consent screen. AEGIS uses this access solely to send dealer-approved email from that account. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Control and deletion

Users can disconnect Gmail from AEGIS and can revoke access at any time in their Google Account. Dealers may request export or deletion of their workspace data.
