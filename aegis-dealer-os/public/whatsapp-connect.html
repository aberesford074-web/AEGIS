Connect WhatsApp

Sign in with Meta and choose the WhatsApp Business number this dealership uses. AEGIS handles the technical setup.

1. Sign in to Meta
2. Choose or create the business account
3. Select the WhatsApp number
4. Return automatically to AEGIS
AEGIS stores the connection securely. Unknown WhatsApp senders are not allowed to control business data until a dealer manager pairs them.
