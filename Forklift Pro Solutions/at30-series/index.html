Applications

Designed for the surfaces that stop ordinary pallet trucks.

Standard pallet trucks are built for smooth floors. The AT30 is built for gravel, broken tarmac, muddy yards and construction tracks.

  • Builders' merchants moving block, stone and timber packs
  • Farm yards handling feed, supplies and outdoor pallets
  • Construction sites crossing temporary tracks and uneven ground
  • Industrial yards handling heavy loads in poor weather

Meet the lineup

Three configurations. One heavy-duty all-terrain platform.

Pure Off-Road Capability

AEGIS AT30 Core

Open-frame front chassis with 100mm clearance for gravel, mud and uneven construction tracks without low-hanging obstructions.

Best for: construction sites, unpaved farm yards, timber pads, and temporary outdoor venues.

Impact-Guarded Efficiency

AEGIS AT30 Guarded

Wraparound steel shroud protects the drive assembly and wiring in busy yards, masonry bays and factory areas.

Best for: paved concrete industrial yards, stone masons, factory bays, and busy builders' merchants.

High-Frequency Ride-On Crossover

AEGIS AT30PS Pilot

Fold-down operator platform for longer site runs, folding upright again for tight pedestrian handling.

Best for: multi-acre distribution terminals, airport logistics hubs, and sprawling supply depots.

Built-in operator safety

3-tonne power. Pedestrian control built in.

Layered mechanical and electronic safety systems help operators stay in control under 3-tonne loads.

01

Emergency Reverse Switch

Stops reverse drive and moves the truck away if the tiller contacts the operator.

02

Dead-Man Handle Braking

Releases, vertical tiller movement, or loss of grip trigger automatic braking.

03

Protected Drive Chassis

Steel chassis helps shield the drive wheel and reduces foot exposure.

04

Controlled Walking Speed

Limited travel speed improves control and reaction time under load.
