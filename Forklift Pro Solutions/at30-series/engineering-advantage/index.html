AEGIS AT30 technical buyer guide

Engineered for UK sites. Built beyond standard pallet trucks.

The AEGIS AT30 is engineered for a category of outdoor material handling work poorly served by standard indoor pallet trucks: heavy loads, poor ground conditions, wet weather, and demanding commercial use.

01

Why 3 tonnes matters

Many all-terrain pallet trucks in the UK market sit around 1,200kg to 1,500kg capacity. The AT30's 3,000kg rating gives builders merchants, timber yards, stone suppliers, agricultural operations, and industrial sites real headroom for dense, difficult loads.

02

Why 100mm clearance matters

Standard indoor trucks often bottom out on broken tarmac, gravel transitions, thresholds, mud, and temporary tracks. The AT30's high-clearance chassis and large traction wheel are designed to keep moving where low-slung warehouse trucks scrape, stall, or become immobilized.

Built for British conditions

Wet weather, grit and winter cold are engineering problems.

Outdoor sites punish ordinary electric pallet trucks. Water ingress, grit contamination, brittle wiring, and exposed components are common causes of breakdowns. The AT30 platform is configured around those realities.

Four-proof throttle switch

Waterproof, dustproof, shockproof and burn-resistant control hardware helps reduce failures caused by rain, grit and heavy site use.

-40°C silicone wiring

Upgraded silicone wiring remains flexible in sub-zero winter conditions, reducing cracking risk compared with standard brittle PVC looms.

Commercial hydraulic system

A heavy-duty 50mm reinforced piston rod, anti-jamming design and protective cylinder guard help resist bending, warping and premature hydraulic failure.

350x100mm traction wheel

The oversized solid rubber drive wheel gives the machine more contact, grip and obstacle tolerance across uneven outdoor surfaces.

Built better than standard off-road pallet trucks

Small engineering details that reduce downtime.

The AT30 platform is packed with practical, field-focused upgrades that matter when trucks are working outside, carrying heavy loads, and getting punished by mud, grit, water and uneven surfaces.

01Fully sealed gearbox

Large-modulus gearing, synthetic grease and sealed construction help improve service life in dusty and wet conditions.

02Dust-proof steering bearing cover

Added protection helps keep water, sand and debris away from the steering bearing for smoother tiller control.

03Emergency lifting button

Provides a practical backup function if control faults interrupt normal lifting operation.
